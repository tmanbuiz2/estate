# NB and Canada compliance research register

Research baseline: 2026-10-27. This is a planning register, not an exhaustive opinion, legal certification or a claim that every source has been noted up. Recheck consolidations, proclamations, amendments, regulations and relevant decisions before a live pilot. Entries below distinguish law, guidance, platform policy and engineering choices. Their applicability depends on business activities, property/tenancy type and user jurisdiction.

## Engineering commitments already implemented

- Sample listings are unmistakably fictional. No false assurance of available housing.
- Costs distinguish confirmed extras, estimates, included items and unknown amounts.
- Public API projection excludes owner contact/internal applicant fields.
- All API writes fail closed. No identity files or payment credentials are accepted.
- UI explicitly says local drafts are not bookings, applications, reports or landlord notifications.
- A local erase control removes browser demo records.

These controls reduce scope; they do not establish legal compliance of a future live service.

## Unresolved areas and scope limits

This register does not resolve all case law, contractual terms, licences, insurance, business registration, tax reporting, defamation/content liability, legal-practice restrictions, employment obligations or every municipal rule. Assess additional financial-services rules before adding loans, rent guarantees, insurance referrals or investments. Short-term accommodation has separate municipal/tax/platform implications and is outside the initial product scope. Expansion beyond NB needs a separate jurisdiction matrix (including provincial privacy/language/tenancy rules), not a single Canada-wide legal switch.

## Change process

Each live feature needs an owner, source/section, coverage/exceptions, effective date, review date, implementation link and verification evidence. Sources must be rechecked before launch and material feature changes. The `rule_versions` table is a schema foundation, not an approved legal decision engine. No recurring legal monitoring service has been configured.
